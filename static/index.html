What Maya can and cannot do

Maya can help with

  • General information about autism in the UK
  • Step-by-step guidance on EHCP, PIP, DLA, and other benefits
  • Information about Hounslow and national support services
  • Signposting to NHS, NAS, IPSEA, Gov.UK, and local services
  • Transition to adulthood and adult social care information
  • Carer support and assessments

Maya cannot provide

  • Medical diagnoses or treatment advice — speak to your GP or NHS 111
  • Legal advice for individual cases — contact IPSEA or Citizens Advice
  • Financial advice — contact a qualified adviser
  • Safeguarding or social work decisions
  • Emergency support — call 999 or Samaritans 116 123

Maya uses information from NHS, Gov.UK, National Autistic Society, IPSEA, and Hounslow Council. Always verify important information with the original source before acting on it.

Privacy notice

What information Maya collects

Maya records the length of questions asked and the identifiers of knowledge sources retrieved. It does not store the text of your question, your name, IP address, or any other personal information.

Feedback submissions record only the issue type and whether a comment was provided — not the comment text itself.

What Maya does not do

  • Maya does not create user accounts or session histories.
  • Maya does not share data with third parties.
  • Maya does not use cookies or tracking technologies.
